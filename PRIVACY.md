# Privacy

This project has no analytics, advertising identifiers, background telemetry or maintainer-operated cloud service.

Mock mode remains local. Live mode sends the script, selected avatar/voice identifiers and generation settings directly to the configured provider. The provider may process and retain data under its own privacy notice and account settings; the maintainers of this repository do not receive those requests.

API keys, private avatar identifiers, voice identifiers, training media, generated videos and job metadata must remain in environment variables or `.runtime/` and are excluded from Git.

Do not submit confidential, regulated or third-party personal data unless you have a valid legal basis, provider authorization and appropriate consent. Delete local outputs when no longer required. Review every generated video before publication.
