# Responsible Use Policy

Use this project only with identities, voices, media and accounts that you own or are explicitly authorized to use.

## Allowed

- Tutorials, training, product education, internal communication and lawful marketing.
- Generating an operator's own authorized Digital Twin or a licensed stock avatar.
- Clearly disclosed synthetic media and branded demonstrations.

## Not allowed

- Impersonation, fraud, harassment, deceptive endorsements or fabricated evidence.
- Cloning or using another person's face or voice without authorization.
- Political, medical, financial, legal or emergency claims presented as a real person's statement without proper review and disclosure.
- Evading provider restrictions, consent checks, account controls or content safeguards.
- Publishing content that violates applicable law, privacy rights, publicity rights, copyright, trademark or provider terms.

## Required safeguards

- Keep identity use behind explicit human approval.
- Disclose synthetic or AI-generated presentation when context could otherwise mislead viewers.
- Review pronunciation, factual claims, captions and visual context before publication.
- Stop provider execution when consent, ownership or authorization is uncertain.
